It began with one bicycle.

In 2011, Sunita Verma — a schoolteacher in Sitapur — noticed that girls vanished from her Class 7 register every monsoon. The road to the high school flooded; the walk was long and unsafe.

She raised ₹4,200 from her colleagues and bought a second-hand bicycle for one student, Pooja. Pooja now teaches at the same school. Ujala grew from that one ride.

Fifteen years, one road

2011One bicycle, one girl, Sitapur.

What we believe

Our work is shaped by girls and their mothers. These promises guide every decision.

Girls leadA girls' council in every district sets our priorities each year.
Families, not just studentsWe sit with fathers and mothers as much as with daughters.
Dignity in every imageNo pity photos. Every story is shared with consent.
Open booksAudited accounts and FCRA returns published every year.
