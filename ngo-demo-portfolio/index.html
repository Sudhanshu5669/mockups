Shared across all four

Bilingual by defaultEvery font pairing supports Devanagari. The EN / हिं toggle sits in the header; Hindi pages are fully set, not machine-translated.
Trust up frontRegistration, FCRA number, audited fund breakdown and annual reports are always one click from the Donate button.
Giving the Indian wayUPI QR and AutoPay first, then cards and net banking. Monthly giving and sponsorship are presented as the default.
Dignified imageryPortraits with names and voices, never pity photos. AI-generated demo imagery illustrates each cause. People, names and stories are fictional; replace with consented NGO photography for a live website.
